NailGrow Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026

In short: NailGrow does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your photos and your history stay on your device and are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Who we are

NailGrow is published by Žan Konečnik, an individual developer based in Slovenia (European Union). You can reach us at nailgrow.support@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in NailGrow is stored only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • the photos you take of your hands, and smaller copies used as thumbnails;
  • the dates of your photos, your streaks and any slips you log;
  • notes and reflections you choose to write;
  • your settings, such as reminder time, theme and which hands you track.

We never receive any of it. The app has no server to send it to. Deleting the app deletes this data.

If you use your phone's own backup (for example iCloud Backup or a backup to a computer), that backup may include NailGrow's data, as it does for most apps. Those backups are controlled by you and your device's provider, not by us. On Android, NailGrow opts out of the automatic Google backup.

Permissions

  • Camera — to take your daily photos. The camera is used only while the capture screen is open.
  • Notifications — only if you turn on the daily reminder. Reminders are scheduled on your device; no notification is sent from a server.
  • Add to photo library — only when you choose to save a comparison or time-lapse. NailGrow can add images to your library but does not read it.

Moving to a new phone

If you use "Move to a new phone", NailGrow creates an encrypted file of your history on your device. You decide where to send it (for example to your own cloud storage, email or AirDrop). The key that opens it is shown only as a QR code on your old phone. We never receive the file or the key.

Purchases

NailGrow offers a one-time in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies; we never see your payment details.

If you email us

If you write to our support address, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. The app pre-fills your app version and operating system so we can help; you can see and delete that before sending. We use your email only to reply to you, and you can ask us to delete our correspondence at any time. Our email is hosted by Google (Gmail).

Children

NailGrow is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you have the right to access, correct and delete personal data held about you. Since NailGrow holds none, the only data we could have is an email you sent us — write to us and we will delete it. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; in Slovenia that is the Information Commissioner (Informacijski pooblaščenec).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date at the top. If the app ever starts handling any personal data, this page will say exactly what and why before that version is released.
